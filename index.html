Move with the left stick; push it fully outward to sprint. CAREER MODE: you control one player only. Buttons change with the play: Pass/Shoot/Through/Cross and a Skill move when you have the ball (hold Pass, Shoot or Through to charge power; release on the green line for a perfect pass), Call/Volley/Run/Cross when your team has it, and Press/Tackle/Slide/Clear when defending. Use the VIEW button for 3rd person, 1st person, broadcast or stadium cameras. Matchday arrival: use the left stick to walk through the chanting plaza, gate, atrium and upper concourse to the dressing room; drag the open world area to look around; the yellow button opens nearby interactions and wayfinding. Push up to move forward. Fouls, cards, penalties and offside are live. Broadcast camera now follows play smoothly from inside the stadium without snapping to the receiver. Tap II to pause, change formation or camera. On the pitch, drag the screen to orbit the camera 360° and see where the ball is; it recentres a moment after you let go. Best in landscape.
